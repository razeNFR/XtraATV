# Xtra TV 1.2.5

### New
- **Proxy button** in the player (hidden at first, Settings > Player buttons): OK turns the custom proxy on or off and the stream starts again with it, OK held opens the list of the proxies (turn each one on or off, move it up or down). It is the same list as in the settings, and "All the proxy settings…" opens them.
- **The ambiance color everywhere**: the color chosen in the Xtra TV settings is now the color of accent of the app (loading circles, timeline, the icon and the name of the focused button, the tab shown, sliders, switches, the multi-stream row).
- **Change page without OK** (Xtra TV settings, off at first): tabs and side menu entries show their page as soon as they have the focus. The row of the other streams in the player is not changed.
- **Lists of the settings in side panels**: the tabs (navigation, following, saved, channel, category, search), the favorites and the custom proxies open in a side panel like the proxies of the player: a switch on each row, the house for the tab opened first, up / down arrows to move a row.
- **Custom proxies**: their status (online in green, offline in red) is shown in the panel of the settings and in the one of the player. From the settings, a proxy can also be added, edited or deleted there, and the full screen is still one click away.
- **Update popup**: it shows the changelog of the new version (it scrolls with up / down), "Update" has the focus, or "Ignore".

### Changes
- **Stream info when switching**: switching streams (left / right or from the row of the other streams) shows only the info of the stream for 2.5 s (title, channel, category, viewers, quality), without the buttons nor the timeline. Back hides it, left / right keep switching, OK gives the whole HUD.
- **The HUD comes back** when a panel opened from it closes (row of the streams, multi-stream, chat, small window, dimming, quality...), with the focus on the button that opened it.
- **"This list" tab**: when the list of the stream is already one of the tabs (Following, Popular, a language, the category), that tab is selected and "This list" is no longer shown.
- **Multi-stream row**: the icon of the multi-stream in the color of accent, smaller cards, the selected streams numbered (their place in the grid), and "Validate" at the start of the tabs.
- **Refresh**: its icon turns in the color of accent, a light sweeps over the cards (on the home too) and a message says it is done. The button is now at the right of the tabs.
- **Popups**: the focus is on the main button ("Yes", "OK"...) at once.
- **"Games" is now "Categories"** everywhere.
- **Player icons**: all filled, "Write in the chat" is a paper plane and "Chat layout" a screen with a side panel, so they no longer look alike. Every Xtra TV button has its icon in Settings > Player buttons.
- **Channel page**: Download, Share and Notifications are hidden at first (Settings > UI > Channel tabs).
- Hidden on TV: the "Material time picker" of the sleep timer (made for touch screens) and the "Live notifications" of Android (the live banner of Xtra TV replaces them, they are turned off).

### Fixes
- **Home**: the focus goes to the first streamer of Following, even when Popular loads first; with nobody live it goes to Popular (or the next row), and it never moves once a key was pressed.
- **Screensavers**: the system (and the screensavers of launchers like Monet that look at it) always sees that a stream plays, also while the ad blocker replaces an ad.

### Install
Download `XtraTV-1.2.5-tv.apk` below and install it over the previous version, your settings and account are kept.
