# Xtra TV 1.0.2

### New
- **Log in with a code**: on TV, log in by typing a code on twitch.tv/activate from your phone or computer, the login finishes by itself. This login does not unlock 1440p: Twitch only gives 1440p to its website, so it needs a token of the website that only the Twitch login page gives. Use the login page if you want 1440p.
- **Back button in a stream**, three choices in the Xtra TV settings: stop the stream, keep it in a **mini player**, or keep **only the sound** while you browse. A **Now playing** card in the side menu brings the stream back or closes it.
- **Follow button** on the channel pages, and **follow / unfollow** in the actions of a stream card (long press OK), next to the channel, its videos and its category.
- **Two new remote shortcuts** (option): while the controls are hidden, up shows the other streams and down shows or hides the chat.
- **Card size**: stream cards per row on the home, and 2 to 6 stream columns in the lists.
- **Ad blocker settings** in their own screen of the Xtra TV settings, with video swap and the VAFT player types on by default.
- **Cleaner settings for TV**: phone-only settings are hidden, and the player button settings show the icon of each button.
- **Fresh lists**: the home refresh loads new thumbnails, stream lists load again when you come back to them, and streamers who went offline leave the switch list.

### Install
Download `XtraTV-1.0.2-tv.apk` below and install it over the previous version, your settings and account are kept.
