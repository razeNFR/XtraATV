# Xtra TV 1.3.5

### What's new
- **Multi-stream video quality profiles & per-stream override**: prevent lag and frame drops on TVs that struggle decoding 4 simultaneous `720p60` streams by choosing a **Multi-stream quality** profile (**Potato**: `720p` / `480p` / `360p`, **Low**: `1080p` / `720p` / `360p`, **Medium**: `1080p` / `720p` / `480p`, **High** *(default)*: `Max` / `720p` / `720p`, **Maximum**: `Max` on all streams, or **Custom** with independent caps for 2, 3, and 4 streams from `160p` to `Max`). Accessible both in *Xtra TV settings → Player* and live inside Multi-Stream (**OK → Video quality**), where you can also override the resolution of a single specific stream (`This stream`) without changing the others.

### Install
Download `XtraTV-1.3.5-tv.apk` below and install it over the previous version, your settings and account are kept.
