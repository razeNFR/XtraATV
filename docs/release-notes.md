# Xtra TV 1.0.1

### New
- **Timeline thumbnails on live streams**: thumbnails now show while seeking on a live stream and when going back in time into its VOD. Twitch only makes them once the broadcast is over, so they are taken from the video itself.
- **Two new chat displays**: next to the stream (left or right) and above or under it, down to a single line that scrolls from right to left. The chat layout panel adapts to the chosen display and has a new text size slider.
- **Live previews in the stream switcher banner** of the player.
- **Next / previous streamer overlay**: when switching streamer with the remote shortcuts, the previous and next streamers show on the sides of the screen for a few seconds.
- **Sound in the live previews**, on by default, can be turned off in the settings. Previews also start faster (360p, ad-free).
- **Xtra TV settings**: every option added by Xtra TV is now in one place, in a new screen of the app settings.
- **Automatic update check**: the app looks for a new version once a day (can be turned off in the update settings).

### Install
Download `XtraTV-1.0.1-tv.apk` below and install it over 1.0.0, your settings and account are kept.
