# Xtra TV 1.4.6.5

### New & Polish
- **Reorganized *Settings > Xtra TV* into 5 zero-scroll sub-menus + shortcuts**: grouped the Xtra TV settings into **Home & Side menu**, **Remote & Navigation**, **TV Player & Multi-stream**, **Previews & Live alerts**, and **Languages & Content** so every screen fits cleanly without long scrolling, added direct cross-links inside **Settings > Player** and **Settings > Interface**, and preserved instant Settings Search across every option with full breadcrumb paths.
- **Reorderable *Following* shortcuts in *Settings > Interface > Navigation panel***: added **Up / Down (`↑ / ↓`)** ordering arrows on **Videos**, **Channels**, and **Categories** under *Following* so you can arrange their exact order in the left side menu.
- **Configurable *Stream info header size* in the Player HUD**: added **Stream info header size** (**Normal (default)**, **Medium**, **Large**, **Extra large**) in **TV Player & Multi-stream** to scale the streamer's name, favorite star, category badge, box art, channel avatar, and stream title at the top of the HUD while keeping safe spacing from the top-right clock.
- **Toggleable *Favorite star* in *Player buttons***: added a **Favorite star** switch right under **Channel** in **Settings > Player > Player buttons** so you can show or hide the `☆ / ★` favorite capsule in the live player HUD header.
- **Background audio on app exit disabled by default**: changed **Keep stream playing when leaving the app** to **Never** by default (and applied a one-time reset to **Never** on update to `1.4.6.5`; can still be re-enabled anytime in **Settings > Player > TV Player & Multi-stream**).
- **Seamless Left/Right stream zapping & *Other stream* focus fix**: updated the Left/Right neighbor pills, PiP windows, and stream info HUD to update in place without blinking or rebuilding on consecutive switches (while preserving rapid zapping protection so focus never lands on the timeline), populated the HUD header immediately before stream connection finishes, and fixed focus getting trapped behind the player when switching to a slow or stalled stream from the *Other stream* banner.
- **Side menu navigation fix from *Following* shortcuts**: fixed switching from **Videos**, **Channels**, or **Categories** (*Following*) back to **Home**, **Popular**, or other root sections in the side menu (both on instant hover and on pressing `OK`).
