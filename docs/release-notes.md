# Xtra TV 1.4.5

### New
- **Pinned chat messages (`📌`)**: displays active Twitch pinned chat messages in a compact non-focusable top banner in chat, with real-time updates and a new **Pinned messages** selector (*Hidden* / *Temporary (20 s)* / *Always*) placed right at the top of the TV *Chat layout* panel above **Predictions (chat hidden)** and **Chat delay**.
- **5 & 6 simultaneous Multi-Stream**: extended Multi-Stream from 4 up to **6 simultaneous streams** with a full-screen 16:9 **Mosaic mode** by default (`2/3 × 2/3` main stream + five `1/3 × 1/3` cells around it with zero black bars, and live chat in the 6th cell when watching 5 streams), instant switching between **Mosaic**, **`3 × 2` Grid**, and gap-free **Focus mode**, per-count quality caps, and automatic decoder fallback/step-down.
- **Double Picture-in-Picture (2 floating small windows)**: open up to **2 simultaneous floating small windows** over the full-screen player, with independent corner/size settings, automatic vertical stacking, automatic pause/restore when switching the main player onto and away from a PiP streamer, 4 audio modes (*Main stream*, *Window 1*, *Window 2*, *Mix all* with independent volume sliders), an interactive D-pad window selector, and dynamic toast hints reflecting your configured remote shortcuts; plus a refined TV **minimized player** with symmetric margins, rounded corners, drop shadow, and a discreet ambient-color border that smoothly follows dynamic accent changes in real time.
- **Stream Together (Co-stream) 1-click, `+N` badges & Shared Chat badges**: detects Twitch *Stream Together* (`collaboration`), Guest Star, official event co-streams, Shared Chat sessions (both EventSub and IRC), and live `@mention` co-streams — displaying Twitch-style **`+1` / `+2`…** badges next to streamer names on Home, Stream Lists, and the Streamer Switcher as well as on the player's **Multi-Stream** button, launching all co-streamers in Multi-Stream or opening a partner in a small window in 1 click, plus optional circular source-channel badges in Shared Chat.
- **Channel Points store & Twitch Drops progress**: new **Points & Drops** player button (`tv_ic_diamond`) and remote shortcut (with top auto-focus on load and a pinned **Close** button at the bottom) showing live Channel Points balance, 1-click bonus chest claim, custom rewards redemption (with message prompt when required), and active Twitch Drops separated between the current stream's category and other account campaigns in progress (automatically hiding claimed drops).
- **OLED screen protection & End-of-stream behavior**: optional **OLED screen protection** dims static overlays (clock, chat, badges) and subtly pixel-shifts every 45 s after 3 minutes of remote inactivity; new **When live stream ends** setting (with raid priority notice) lets everyone choose between returning to Home, staying on the player, or auto-switching to another live followed channel — falling back to a live stream in the same category — after a 10 s cancellable countdown.
- **Instant 30s Clip creation & "Open on phone" QR code**: new **Create clip (30s)** player button and action generating a Twitch clip and displaying its QR code to trim/title/share on your phone, plus QR code sharing for streams, VODs (with exact timestamp), and clips.
- **Background sync for Android TV Launcher (*Followed Live*)**: periodic background `WorkManager` sync keeps the Android TV / Google TV *Followed Live* channel and *Watch Next* row fresh even while Xtra TV is closed.
- **Instant Multi-Account Twitch switcher & Guest mode**: save multiple Twitch accounts on the same TV, switch between them or **Guest mode** in 1 click from the side menu without revoking tokens, with per-account Favorites, Statistics, and Home row layouts.
