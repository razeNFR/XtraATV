# Xtra TV 1.2.0

### New
- **Favorite channels**: hold OK on a stream and choose "Add to favorites". When they are live, your favorites come first in the followed streams (home row, Followed page, other streams of the player), in your own order, then the others by viewers. A star shows them on the home, and their order can be changed in the Xtra TV settings.
- **Rows by language on the home**: pick one or more languages in the Xtra TV settings to get a row of the popular streams for each of them, under Popular.
- **Switch lists in the other streams row**: while watching, press up from the cards of the other streams to reach tabs: this list, followed, popular, your language rows and the category of the stream. Picking a stream from another tab makes it the list of left / right. The tabs are also there when picking the streams of a multi-stream.
- **Small window**: hold OK on a stream of the other streams row to watch it in a small window at the top right, while the sound of the main stream goes on. Hold OK again to swap the two streams, press back to close it.
- **Focus mode in the multi-stream**: with 3 or 4 streams, one stream big with its chat at its right and the others small under it. Press OK on a small stream to put it big, hold OK to open its panel. Turn it on or off from the panel of a stream.
- **More entries in the side menu**: shortcuts to the videos, the channels or the categories of the followed channels can be added in the Xtra TV settings. They show under Following, smaller, and are checked in Interface > Navigation tabs, under Following.
- **New account page**: the two ways of logging in side by side (code on twitch.tv/activate, Twitch login page) with what each one gives and lacks and whether it is done. Each one can be done or done again at any time without losing the other, and a log out button removes everything.
- **Greeting and time at the top of the home**: "Hello" (or "Good evening") with your name and picture, and the time; the time is also at the right of the title of the other screens.
- **Buttons of the channel page**: Download, Share and Notifications can each be hidden in Interface > Channel tabs.
- **The other streams row loads more** as you go to its end, 30 streams at a time, and left / right in the player follow it.
- **Version number in the settings**, under the name of the screen.

### Changes
- **Card sizes by name**: the size of the cards on the home and in the lists now reads very big, big, medium, small or very small.
- **Opaque side menu**: the texts of the screen behind no longer show through it.
- **Other streams button** on every stream of the player, the row has its tabs even for a stream opened alone.
- **Tabs of the other streams row** change the list when OK is pressed, with a loading shown.
- **Simpler Interface settings**: the display switches are gone, their options are on for everyone (previews of the streams, round pictures, compact numbers, uptime, tags, tabs pages, time left, recent searches...). "Activate notifications when following" stays.
- **Grayed out settings** can be reached with the remote again, OK on them tells what turns them on.

### Fixes
- A tab hidden in the settings no longer leaves its page in the next tab (videos shown in the channels tab).
- The side menu shortcuts open their own tab (even when it is hidden in the settings) and stay selected.
- Popular and the language rows load in the other streams row, and the category there uses the languages chosen for that category.
- Left / right pressed fast or held in the other streams row stay in the row and move smoothly.
- The star of a favorite shows at once on the Followed page, on the right channel.

### Install
Download `XtraTV-1.2.0-tv.apk` below and install it over the previous version, your settings and account are kept.
