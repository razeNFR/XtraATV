# Xtra TV 1.2.7

### New
- **Statistics page**: OK on the card of the statistics of the home opens them in detail: 7 days, 30 days or since the start, the time watched by day, the streamers and the categories most watched, when you watch (a grid of the days and the hours), your records (longest session, busiest day, streak of days), the ads blocked by week and the proxies used. "Reset" erases them. The new statistics (by day, by category, by hour) are counted from this version.
- **Channel page**: the banner of the channel in big at the top, the avatar with a red ring when the channel is live, the name, the followers, the title and the category, and the buttons over it. It folds down to a single line when you go down to the videos.
- **Settings in two columns**: the categories on the left (Xtra TV, Player, Chat, Interface, Ads and proxies, Downloads, Updates and backup, Advanced), their settings on the right. Nothing is lost, the values you chose are kept.
  - **Ads and proxies** groups the ad blocker (moved out of the Xtra TV settings), the custom proxies and the HTTP proxies. The custom proxies are one row ("On · 3 proxies"), their panel has the on / off switch at the top.
  - Settings made for phones are hidden on TV (cutouts of the screen, audio when the screen locks).

### Changes
- **Categories**: wide cards, the whole box art on the left, the name, the viewers and the streams on the right, 4 by row. Nothing is cut anymore.
- **Followed channels**: big avatars, 6 by row. The channels live come first (red ring, LIVE, category and viewers), your favorites first in each part (with a star), the last live in "2 hours ago" style and "Followed since…". "Account / Local" is gone, a small TV marks a channel followed only on this TV.
- **Live duration** on the cards of the home ("2 h 39"), the same short badge in the lists of streams.
- **Sort button**: shorter ("Viewers ↓" instead of "Sort by: Viewers (descending)"), at the same place.
- **Title in the player**: smaller, still easy to read.
- **Chat delay**: Auto by default.
- **Settings**: "UI" is now "Interface", "Navigation tabs" is now "Navigation panel". The back and search buttons get the white disc and the ambiance color when focused.
- **Translations**: about 80 texts that were still in English (proxies, video swap, login, 7TV...) are translated in every language.

### Install
Download `XtraTV-1.2.7-tv.apk` below and install it over the previous version, your settings and account are kept.
