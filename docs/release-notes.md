# Xtra TV 1.2.2

### Fixes
- **Switching streams during a blocked ad**: going left / right quickly while the ad blocker was replacing an ad could show the stream of another streamer, then make the app close. Each switch now stops everything the ad blocker started for the previous stream.
- **Ads at the start of a stream**: an ad at the start of a stream (pre-roll) is caught before any of it is shown, the ad blocker starts at once and the picture of the stream stays until the video without the ad comes.

### Install
Download `XtraTV-1.2.2-tv.apk` below and install it over the previous version, your settings and account are kept.
