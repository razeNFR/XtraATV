# XtraPlus 1.4.9.1

### Fixes & Improvements
- **Smoother Adblock (Swap+) Transitions**:
  - Eliminated audio/video rollbacks and stutters when entering and exiting pre-roll and mid-roll ad breaks.
  - Improved stability on **HEVC / 1440p** channels by matching video formats between streams to avoid decoder switches and unexpected fallbacks.
  - Reduced pre-roll ads when opening channels with **Swap+** enabled (Classic Swap remains unchanged).
  - Stopped background refreshes immediately when switching channels to save bandwidth and battery during fast zapping.
- **DMCA-Muted VOD Audio Recovery Fixed**:
  - Fixed muted VOD audio recovery so original audio now plays properly on recovered (green) sections of the seek bar.
  - Improved reliability when switching video quality and kept recovered sections cleanly highlighted in green on the progress bar.
