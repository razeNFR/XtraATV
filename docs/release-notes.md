# Xtra TV 1.3.7

### What's new
- **Send chat messages directly from Multi-Stream**: press **OK** on any stream tile (`2`, `3`, or `4` streams, even when that stream's chat is hidden) and choose **Write a message** to open the TV chat composer with voice input, emotes, and automatic Twitch room restriction checks (locked icon + localized toast for follower-only, minimum follow duration, subscriber-only, slow mode, and emote-only rooms).

### Fixes & stability
- **Eliminated random player HUD & focus loss (#68)**: added an active focus shield on the full-screen player (Live, VOD, and Clips) preventing background screens or late-loading Home rows from stealing focus behind the player, restored player focus automatically when returning from Android TV screensavers or system overlays, and hardened HUD fade animations so interrupted transitions can never leave the controls invisible.

### Install
Download `XtraTV-1.3.7-tv.apk` below and install it over the previous version, your settings and account are kept.
