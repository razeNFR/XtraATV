# Xtra TV 1.4.6.4

### New & Polish
- **Redesigned tabbed PiP & Chat side panels with icons (zero scroll)**: reorganized the **Chat** (**Placement** · **Appearance** · **Options**) and **PiP** (**Display** · **Audio**) side panels with instant-hover top tabs (switching immediately on D-pad Left/Right without pressing OK), clear vector icons on every option, and a pinned 1-row horizontal action footer so every option fits without vertical scrolling.
- **Interactive D-pad *Free move…* for PiP & Chat**: added a **Free move…** option directly below **Position** in both the PiP and Chat panels — hides the HUD and lets you nudge or glide any PiP window or the Chat overlay anywhere on screen with the D-pad (`OK` saves, `Back` cancels and restores the previous position).
- **Per-window PiP opacity (`5 %` – `100 %`)**: added an **Opacity** slider (`5 %` to `100 %` in `5 %` steps, independent for Window 1 and Window 2) in the PiP **Display** tab with live preview.
- **Compact single-line *Now playing* bar in the Side Menu**: replaced the tall 16:9 *Now playing* preview card between *Search* and *Home* with a sleek single-row bar (`[ live avatar + streamer name ]` + inline `[ ✕ ]` stop button on the right) so opening the menu while a stream is minimized no longer pushes or scrolls the menu entries.
- **Smart `20 %` ghost opacity on the minimized player (zero movement)**: the minimized player stays anchored in the bottom-right corner and smoothly fades to **`20 %` opacity** whenever a focused card sits behind it (on Home or any grid) as well as throughout **Settings** and **Statistics**, returning to `100 %` automatically when focus moves away.
- **Favorite star capsule in the Live Player HUD header**: added a D-pad focusable **`☆ / ★` Favorite** capsule right next to the streamer's name on live streams so you can add or remove the streamer from your Favorites in one click without leaving full-screen playback.
- **PiP symmetric edge margins & HUD-synced auto-dimming chrome**: replaced the asymmetric overscan PiP padding with symmetric 4-side margins and added **Edge margin** (**Reduced** `14dp` by default, **Normal** `26dp`, or **Flush** `0dp`) plus **Streamer name** (**Auto (with HUD)** by default, **Always visible**, or **Hidden**) — after 4 seconds without the HUD, the streamer badge fades out and the white PiP border smoothly thins down to a subtle `1dp` outline.
- **Real-world local clock time on the VOD & Live/DVR timeline scrub pill**: added the real-world local broadcast time (e.g. `-15:00 · 01:45:00 · 🕒 21:45`, formatted in your TV's timezone and 12h/24h setting) inside the timeline seek preview pill on VODs and live-rewind DVR broadcasts.
- **Assignable *Open live alert* remote shortcut & dynamic banner hint**: promoted **Open live alert** to a standalone assignable D-pad / custom remote shortcut (defaulting to **Up (long press)** while preserving any existing custom binding) — the live notification banner dynamically displays whichever key is bound to it, and hides the hint pill without intercepting D-pad Up when unbound.
- **Follow / Unfollow categories from long-press (logged in & logged out)**: added **Follow / Unfollow** directly in the long-press action menu of every category card (*Home*, *Categories*, *Search*, and *Followed categories*), using your Twitch account when signed in or local category follows when signed out.
- **Stream switcher & Multi-stream picker refinements**: highlighted the *Hold OK for PiP* hint with an accented pill badge, refined stream card sizing in the *Other stream* banner, and routed D-pad Up in the Multi-stream picker directly to **Validate** as soon as at least one streamer is selected.
- **Cleaner VOD card long-press menu**: removed *Open on phone (QR)* and *Create clip (30s)* from VOD card long-press menus (*Continue watching*, *Latest videos from followed channels*, and video grids), keeping those actions strictly inside the active player.
- **Navigation & focus fixes**: fixed pressing Back inside a game category or channel closing the app instead of returning to the previous screen, fixed overlapping root screens when leaving a category via the side menu, and refined focus retention across Chat and PiP panels.
