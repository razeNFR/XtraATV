# Xtra TV 1.4.6.4

### New & Polish
- **Favorite star capsule in the Live Player HUD header**: added a D-pad focusable **`☆ / ★` Favorite** capsule right next to the streamer's name on live streams so you can add or remove the streamer from your Favorites in one click without leaving full-screen playback.
- **PiP symmetric edge margins & HUD-synced auto-dimming chrome**: replaced the asymmetric overscan PiP padding with symmetric 4-side margins and added **Edge margin** (**Reduced** `14dp` by default, **Normal** `26dp`, or **Flush** `0dp`) plus **Streamer name** (**Auto (with HUD)** by default, **Always visible**, or **Hidden**) to the PiP settings panel — after 4 seconds without the player HUD, the streamer badge fades out and the white PiP border smoothly thins down to a subtle `1dp` outline, reappearing automatically whenever the HUD opens.
- **Real-world local clock time on the VOD & Live/DVR timeline scrub pill**: added the real-world local broadcast time (e.g. `-15:00 · 01:45:00 · 🕒 21:45`, formatted in your TV's timezone and 12h/24h setting) inside the timeline seek preview pill on VODs and live-rewind DVR broadcasts.
- **Assignable *Open live alert* remote shortcut & dynamic banner hint**: promoted **Open live alert** to a standalone assignable D-pad / custom remote shortcut (defaulting to **Up (long press)** while preserving any existing custom binding) — the live notification banner now dynamically displays whichever key is bound to it, and cleanly hides the key hint pill without intercepting D-pad Up when no key is assigned.
- **Cleaner VOD card long-press menu**: removed *Open on phone (QR)* and *Create clip (30s)* from VOD card long-press menus (*Continue watching*, *Latest videos from followed channels*, and video grids), keeping those actions strictly inside the active player.

Download `XtraTV-1.4.6.4-tv.apk` below and install it over the previous version, your settings and account are kept.
