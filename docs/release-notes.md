# Xtra TV 1.3.0

### What's new
- **Featured live banner on Home**: optional hero banner at the top of the home screen with soft 4-edge fade into the background and quick *Watch* / *Channel* actions.
- **Dynamic ambiance color**: new *Dynamic* ambiance mode that smoothly adapts the background glow to the focused stream or channel banner.
- **Compact side rail**: optional slim icon rail on the left when the side menu closes instead of hiding it completely.
- **Channel page redesign & Schedule tab**: soft-feathered channel header banner, TV-friendly *About* tab with bio, Twitch panels, QR codes for social links, and a new *Schedule* tab for upcoming streams.
- **Unified TV long-press menus**: long-pressing OK on VODs, Clips, and Bookmarks now opens the TV action panel (*Watch*, *Resume*, *Watch from start*, *Bookmark*, *Channel*, *Category*, *Download*).
- **VOD chapters**: chapter markers on the timeline, chapter name in the seek preview, and a TV side panel to jump between categories/chapters.
- **Clips autoplay & Channel +/−**: optional automatic playback of the next clip in the list when a clip ends, plus instant clip skipping with Channel + / −.
- **Visual emote picker in chat**: D-pad emote drawer in the chat message composer with *Recent*, *7TV / BTTV / FFZ*, and *Twitch* tabs.
- **Interactive live alerts**: hold D-pad Up while a live notification banner is shown to watch immediately, open in the small window, add to an active multi-stream, or open the channel.
- **Saved multi-stream groups**: save 2–4 streamer combinations from a multi-stream and relaunch the live ones in one click.
- **Android TV Home channels**: publishes *Followed Live* and *Watch Next* (unfinished VODs) directly to the Android TV / Google TV home launcher.

### Install
Download `XtraTV-1.3.0-tv.apk` below and install it over the previous version, your settings and account are kept.
